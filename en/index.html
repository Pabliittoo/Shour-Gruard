SHORE GUARD / FAQ

Before
we begin.

Answers about terraces, shoreline protection and preparing for the work.

Do you build terraces?+

Yes. SHORE GUARD builds terraces and decking. Prepare site photos, approximate dimensions and a short description of how you would like to use the space.

What determines the cost?+

The terrace dimensions or shoreline length, site conditions, structure, materials and equipment access. Prepare photos, a location and approximate dimensions for an initial assessment.

Should I choose geogrid or gabions?+

It depends on the site. Geogrid suits slope stabilisation; gabions provide a more substantial erosion-control structure. The methods can be combined.

Does the water body need to be drained?+

Not always. Feasibility depends on water levels, access to the bank and the type of structure. These conditions are assessed for each site.

Do you work outside Kyiv?+

Yes. SHORE GUARD works across Ukraine where logistics and site conditions allow the work to be carried out safely.

How long will the reinforcement last?+

Service life depends on the materials, installation and environmental conditions. It is assessed for the specific structure and site.

What should I prepare for a consultation?+

Site photos, the location, approximate terrace dimensions or shoreline length and your expectations for the space.
